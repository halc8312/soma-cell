INTERPRETATION
The strong periodic/long-delay grammar retention seen in 0.6.5 did not reproduce after replacing experimenter-side tournament selection with endogenous shared-world birth/death. Stable ecology did suppress expression, but generation 2 was insufficient for reliable physical loss of the grammar. HGT cannot be credited with grammar retention in these registered runs. This is a negative result, not evidence that neural grammar is universally useless.
