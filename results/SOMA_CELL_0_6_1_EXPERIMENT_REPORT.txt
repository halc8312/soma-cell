1. Development history
----------------------
R1 preregistration (D1.0) failed. Stable safety passed, but the actuator probe never confirmed
fault because a raw energy-debt threshold was incompatible with the P2 physical debt scale.
Those seeds were retired and the failure record remains in the project.

During D1.3, a neural ATP escrow accounting error was found. Small neural repair requests could
leave unused ATP trapped inside neural attachments. Conservation feedback then appeared more
beneficial because it returned this excess ATP. Reusable neural/controller ATP was capped and
assembly ATP was separated from the reusable buffer. The positive-control effect was remeasured
after this correction.

D1.4 changed target selection from absolute motor magnitude to positive work aligned with the
tissue's own current motor command. This avoids suppressing antagonistic cells whose removal may
increase futile drive. No resource coordinate or correct direction is used.

5. Interpretation
-----------------
Supported, narrowly:
After a physically applied actuator loss, the tissue can detect command-to-force loss through
paid repeated probes and autonomously issue a temporary lease that quiesces positively aligned
motor neurons. In the preregistered controlled assay this reduced motor ATP and dissipation and
improved cost-inclusive autopoietic-margin AUC in all 12 pairs.

Not established:
- universal or long-term adaptive benefit
- improved native spatial uptake
- net value of active molecular semantic diagnosis
- net value of the local predictor
- necessity of recurrence
- multigenerational fixation of diagnostic genes
- life, consciousness or open-ended evolution
