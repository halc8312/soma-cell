Purpose
-------
Migrate the 0.6.6 detailed world's gene-coded metabolism and damage/repair
chemistry into independently comparable NumPy/Torch kernels, then integrate
them with the A2 surface/physics layer without deleting phenomena or changing
canonical event and RNG order.

Implemented in the A3 hybrid path
---------------------------------
- generic gene reactions and gene-coded ATP generation
- ATP-paid precursor synthesis, maintenance and surface assembly
- active protein damage and damaged-protein aggregation
- typed aggregate composition for new A3 material plus unresolved legacy mass
- reactive byproducts, membrane oxidation and genome-lesion chemistry
- antioxidant, chaperone, protease, genome and membrane repair
- pure damage-segregation planning
- integrated exactly-once receipts across A2 and A3 cell events
- strict packed-state mask, order, tail, mass, genome and dtype validation

Correctness evidence
--------------------
- A3 dedicated validation: 36 / 36 PASS

Formal A3 benchmark
-------------------
The final source-bound benchmark completed in about 4 hours 3 minutes. It
contains exactly 13 sweep specifications and 65 measurements: worlds
1/8/32/128, cells 1/3/8, particles 216/288/360 and protein capacity
64/128/256. The worlds axis used one warmup and two timed repeats; the other
axes used one timed repeat. CPU and CUDA fp64 correctness probes at 1/10/20
steps all passed before any fp32 timing was admitted.

World-axis median seconds and slowdown relative to frozen 0.6.6 CPU:

worlds  frozen CPU  A3 CPU fp64       A3 CUDA fp64      A3 CUDA fp32
1       0.020599    0.941922 (45.727x) 11.743304 (570.096x) 12.002603 (582.684x)
8       0.164761    7.578641 (45.998x) 96.189753 (583.813x) 94.812362 (575.453x)
32      0.647278   30.482708 (47.094x) 382.072748 (590.276x) 381.561795 (589.486x)
128     2.666772  124.666981 (46.748x) 1528.316903 (573.096x) 1528.881696 (573.308x)

There is no speedup. The correctness-first A3 hybrid performs sequential
per-cell host orchestration, Python object pack/commit work and many small
kernel launches. CUDA utilisation remained low during the run. The structured
per-measurement CUDA peaks were 215,552 allocated bytes for fp64 and 190,976
allocated bytes for fp32, with 2,097,152 reserved bytes for both. External
polling observed up to about 979 MiB total process/device use; that broader
observation is not substituted for the allocator fields in the JSON.

The benchmark fp64 full-world maximum state difference was
3.552713678800501e-15, its material-ledger difference was zero, and RNG and
discrete structure matched exactly. The fp32 candidate full-world maximum
state difference was 3.598134365018738e-06 and its maximum ledger difference
was 1.711311643504132e-06, below the registered candidate bound
7.62939453125e-06. fp32 is not an equivalence or bit-exactness claim.

Pre-implementation A2 RTX baseline
----------------------------------
The frozen A2 baseline on the same RTX 4060 Ti was also slower than the frozen
CPU path: 0.544381729 s CPU versus 3.780191838 s CUDA hybrid for 20 steps, a
6.944009390 runtime ratio. A supplemental recursive full-state check covered
1/10/20 steps with exact RNG/discrete state and equal material residuals. The
A2 values are retained as pre-A3 evidence, not as an A3 speedup comparison.

Scientific limitation
---------------------
A3 is an engineering correctness checkpoint. It does not establish GPU
acceleration, life, consciousness or open-ended evolution. Its measured
performance is substantially slower than the frozen CPU reference, and
full_gpu_world_step remains false.
