SOMA-CELL 0.6.7 R1 PREREGISTRATION INVALIDATION
=================================================

The first preregistration bound source SHA-256
033689f128b328de97f37536646344df50c79cb61e0e653bbf54aaa4e32bcb15.

Before the listed 6711-6713 assays were fully completed, the implementation was
corrected to make translated neural-module protein explicit material, recycle
that material on dormancy/deletion, separate mutation re-entry from HGT
re-entry, and invalidate/restart partial genome copies after HGT insertion.
Those changes altered the source hash. Therefore the 6711-6713 results are
retained as development data only and may not be reported as preregistered
holdout evidence.

A new R2 preregistration uses unseen seeds 6721-6723 and the frozen corrected
source. No R1 seed is reused in R2.
