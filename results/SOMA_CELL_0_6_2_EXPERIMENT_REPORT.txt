Interpretation
--------------
- Explicit ATP/material metering works and conservative cell retirement works.
- The current added P2/formal neural layer does not repay its material cost in the
  preregistered short cue-reversal assay.
- Prediction is not rejected; recurrence is rejected by the predefined screen.
- Full8 retains the previously validated actuator-fault conservation response, but
  even that does not make added neural tissue net-positive in the confirmation task.
- The correct 0.6.2 result is negative: the economically selected amount of *added*
  neural tissue is zero under the tested conditions.
- This is not evidence that neural tissue can never be useful. It motivates the next
  milestone: demand-gated neurogenesis, where neural tissue is built only when its
  expected information value can repay development and maintenance cost.

Not established
---------------
- universal superiority of no neural tissue
- long-term or multi-generation optimum
- net value in novel tasks not tested here
- open-ended evolution, life, consciousness, or subjective experience
