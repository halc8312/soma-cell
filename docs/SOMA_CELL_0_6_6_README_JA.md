# SOMA-CELL 0.6.6

0.6.6 replaces the 0.6.5 accelerated tournament assay with a shared chemical ecology. Organisms reproduce only through physical replication/division, die into corpse chemistry, and may exchange environmental DNA through the inherited HGT path.

## Result
All three main environments reached endogenous generation 2 in 3/3 seeds. Stable active grammar fell to ~1.11/5, but complete grammar absence reached only ~0.22, below the preregistered 0.45 gate. Periodic and long-delay active grammar were ~1.27, only ~0.16 above stable and far below the +0.75 retention gate. Thus the strong complex-environment retention seen in 0.6.5 did not reproduce without experimenter-side tournament selection.
