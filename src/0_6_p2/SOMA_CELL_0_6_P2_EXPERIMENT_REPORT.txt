DESIGN
------
1. Independent whole-life trials:
   6 modes x development seeds 101/202/303 = 18 runs, 100 simulation seconds.
2. Exact-state post-switch common-disturbance twins:
   4 controls x 3 development seeds = 12 pairs.
3. Full vs fixed holdout twins:
   seeds 404/505/606/707/808/909 = 6 pairs.

The development seeds were used while calibrating the physical microfluidic
trap, cue duration and effector scale.  Holdout seeds were not used during that
calibration and are reported separately.

INTERPRETATION
--------------
- The step-indexed common disturbance tape prevents branch-dependent random
  number consumption from creating unrelated future noise.
- The main positive evidence is the 5/6 holdout paired result against the
  equal-material fixed tissue.
- Plasticity has a limited positive signal in the development ablation.
- Prediction learns its target but has no established net body value.
- Recurrence has no established net body value in the present delay task.
- The microfluidic trap is shared physical assay machinery, but its parameters
  were calibrated on the development seeds; those results are not holdout.
- n=6 holdout pairs is a mechanism signal, not statistical generalisation.
- No result establishes consciousness, life status, open-ended evolution or a
  general superiority of larger neural tissue.
